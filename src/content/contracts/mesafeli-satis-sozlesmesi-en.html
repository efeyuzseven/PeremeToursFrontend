ARTICLE 7 – DENTUR AVRASYA REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY AND GENERAL TERMS

7.1. CHECK-IN

In order to complete passport and visa control procedures and to collect his/her ticket, the Passenger must be present at the Dentur Avrasya Stand located in the Teos Marina Customs Building no later than one and a half (1.5) hours prior to the scheduled departure time in order to complete the check-in procedures. Check-in closes thirty (30) minutes prior to the scheduled departure time. During weekends and the high season, Passengers are advised to arrive earlier. Check-in procedures may also be completed at any of the Seller's offices before the date of departure. The Passenger must be on board the ferry at least fifteen (15) minutes before the scheduled departure time. Passengers are responsible for managing the time they spend within the port area and at the duty-free shop in accordance with these requirements.

7.2. OPEN TICKETS

Open tickets may only be confirmed for a scheduled voyage subject to availability and may only be processed by Dentur Avrasya offices. Open tickets must be revalidated within fifteen (15) days from the date on which they are converted into open tickets. An open ticket shall remain valid until the last scheduled voyage of the season in which it was issued. Any open ticket that has not been used by the last scheduled voyage of the relevant season shall automatically become null and void.

7.3. LOSS OF TICKET

A Passenger who loses his/her ticket must purchase a new ticket. The agencies shall not be liable for lost tickets for any reason whatsoever. Any ticket found should be delivered without delay to the Dentur Avrasya Head Office, located at Talatpaşa Boulevard, Kemahlıoğlu No:58.

7.4. NON-TRANSFERABILITY OF THE TICKET

A passenger ticket issued in a specific person's name is non-transferable and may not be resold to any other person. If a ticket is presented by a person other than the individual entitled to travel under that ticket for the purpose of carriage or obtaining a refund, the carrier shall not be liable to the person entitled to travel if, acting in good faith, it has carried the person presenting the ticket or has made the refund to such person.

7.5. TICKET CANCELLATION AND REFUND REQUESTS

Since the provisions set out under Part Three of the Distance Contracts Regulation dated 27 November 2014, titled "Exercise of the Right of Withdrawal and the Obligations of the Parties," do not apply to passenger transportation services, the BUYER does not have a right of withdrawal. Although the Agreement provides that: “Electronic tickets purchased under this Agreement may not, under any circumstances, be amended with respect to the passenger, date, time, or route upon the request of the Seller. The Buyer acknowledges and undertakes that, following confirmation of the sale, the ticket purchased under this Agreement shall not be changed in any manner and no refund shall be requested,” passengers of Dentur Avrasya are nevertheless entitled, provided that they notify the Seller at least three (3) days prior to the scheduled departure, to either (i) place their ticket on open status once and subsequently rebook it for a new date, or (ii) reschedule the ticket once. Requests for refunds of the ticket price shall not be accepted.

7.6. CIRCUMSTANCES WHERE THE PASSENGER IS NOT PERMITTED TO LEAVE TÜRKİYE OR ENTER GREECE

Where the Passenger is prevented from departing from Türkiye or entering Greece due to reasons not attributable to Dentur Avrasya, including but not limited to judicial or administrative restrictions, visa-related issues, or matters relating to a vehicle, pet, or any other item that the Passenger intends to bring with him/her, the contract for carriage shall automatically terminate, and no request for a refund shall be accepted.

7.7. VOYAGE CANCELLATION / CHANGES

7.7.1. Passengers are obliged to register their mobile phone numbers in the system. This enables passengers to be notified of any voyage cancellations or changes.

7.7.2. In the event that a voyage cannot be performed due to adverse weather conditions, the passenger shall be entitled to an unconditional 100% refund. The SELLER shall not be liable for accommodation, food, beverage or similar expenses arising from adverse weather conditions, strikes, lockouts or other circumstances beyond the SELLER's control.

7.7.3. In the event of unscheduled inspections by the Flag State or unforeseen vessel breakdowns, the SELLER reserves the right to change the scheduled departure time and/or substitute the vessel.

7.7.4. The SELLER reserves all rights with respect to the cancellation and/or amendment of any voyage.

7.8. TICKET INSPECTION

Tickets are inspected at the vessel's boarding gate by means of the barcodes displayed during boarding. Passengers holding tickets that have, for any reason, been cancelled, rescheduled or placed on open status shall not be permitted to board the vessel.

7.9. PASSENGERS REQUIRING SPECIAL ASSISTANCE / PASSENGERS TRAVELLING WITH PETS

7.9.1. Travel agencies and passengers must notify the Dentur Avrasya Offices of any passenger requiring special assistance no later than one (1) day prior to departure.
